Text colours that satisfy both gradients

White fails AA on raw #EE6126 (3.29:1) and on raw #1E88E5 (3.68:1), so neither gradient can carry white text on its own. A single constant scrim — rgba(20,12,6,.38) — sits over both gradients at all times. The hue still reads; the luminance is bounded. One ink then clears AA everywhere.

Contrast ratios of sidebar inks against both gradients
Role Hex On orange On blue
Nav label, icon#FFFFFF5.9 : 17.3 : 1
Group heading#EFE3DC5.1 : 16.3 : 1
Active row ink#1A1A1A17.4 : 117.4 : 1
Badge ink#1A1A1A17.4 : 117.4 : 1

Active row and badges sit on an opaque white plate, so their ratio is independent of the gradient underneath — which is why they do not shift during the cross-fade. The section tints are warm or cool whites at 14–26% alpha; none of them is green, amber, red or grey, so no tint can be mistaken for a status.
