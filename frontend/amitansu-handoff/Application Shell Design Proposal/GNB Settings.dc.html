WHY SENTENCES

A labelled field asks the owner to imagine the rule. A sentence states it. He reads left to right, sees the two numbers he can change, and knows exactly what will happen tonight at 2 a.m.

The count underneath is the part that earns trust: it replays the last 30 days of real data against the number now in the box. Set the speed to 50 and watch it jump — that is the moment he understands the threshold, not the label.

Inputs sit on the sentence baseline at 38 px on desktop and 44 px on the phone, so the line stays readable while the target stays tappable.
