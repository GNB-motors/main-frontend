Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

GNB Motors operates GNB Edge, a fleet-management platform used by transport businesses to manage vehicles, trips, fuel, mileage and maintenance records. This policy explains what information we collect from the people who use GNB Edge — organization owners, managers, drivers and field agents — how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices available to you. It covers our web application and our WhatsApp Business messaging channel.

Information we collect

Account and profile. Name, mobile number, email address (optional for drivers), role, and the organization you belong to. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is transformed with an irreversible cryptographic hash (bcrypt) before it is saved, is excluded from every API response, and cannot be viewed by our staff.

Vehicle and trip data. Vehicle registrations, trips, routes, fuel logs, mileage intervals, and maintenance/service records that your organization enters or uploads.

Documents and photos. Fuel-bill and odometer photographs, and vehicle documents, uploaded for record-keeping and automated verification. These images are processed by an OCR pipeline (including a third-party AI vision model) solely to extract figures such as litres, amount, and odometer reading — the pipeline does not use these images for any other purpose.

Location data. For drivers who explicitly grant location permission in the app, we store your most recent location only — one live position per driver, used to power fleet-tracking views for your organization. We do not retain a historical location trail. Revoking permission stops collection going forward.

WhatsApp messaging data. If your organization uses our WhatsApp Business channel to submit fuel bills, we receive the phone number you message us from, the photos and text you send, and delivery/read status. We only process messages from a WhatsApp number after you (or your organization) have explicitly linked it to your GNB Edge account.

Connected third-party accounts. If your organization connects an external telematics account (e.g. FleetEdge), the access credentials are encrypted at rest and are never stored in plain text on your user record.

Technical and log data. Standard request metadata (timestamps, device/browser information) for security and troubleshooting. Our logging is specifically designed to never record passwords, access tokens, message contents, or full phone numbers.

How we use information

To provide the core product: authenticate your account, run fleet/trip/ fuel/mileage tracking, generate reports for your organization, and process document submissions (including those sent over WhatsApp). To send transactional notifications (e.g. document-expiry reminders, submission confirmations) by email, SMS, or WhatsApp. To maintain the security, integrity, and tenant isolation of the platform. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

WhatsApp Business Platform and Meta

Our WhatsApp channel runs on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. When you message our business number, your message content and associated metadata are processed by Meta in addition to us, subject to Meta's own Privacy Policy and WhatsApp Business Policy. We only message a WhatsApp number after it has been opted in by linking it to an active GNB Edge account — we do not send unsolicited messages, and we do not use WhatsApp conversation data for advertising or share it with data brokers. You can unlink your WhatsApp number, or stop the conversation at any time, by contacting your organization administrator or gnbmotors60@gmail.com.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the platform, under contract and only for the purpose of providing their service:

  • Amazon Web Services — file storage and email delivery.
  • Fast2SMS — SMS delivery.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) — message delivery, only for numbers you've linked.
  • Our OCR provider's AI vision model — text extraction from uploaded document photos.
  • FleetEdge/Tata Motors — only if your organization chooses to connect that account.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored or transmitted in plain text. Connected third-party credentials (such as FleetEdge tokens) are encrypted at rest. Data for each organization is logically isolated from every other organization on the platform. Access to the platform is controlled by role-based permissions, and data in transit is encrypted (TLS).

Data retention

We retain account and business records for as long as your organization's account is active, and afterward only as needed to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations. WhatsApp conversation state expires automatically (typically within hours to a few days of inactivity), and delivery logs are retained only briefly for troubleshooting. Live location data is overwritten with each update and is not kept as a history.

Your rights and choices

You may ask your organization administrator or contact us directly to access, correct, or delete your personal information, subject to any retention we're legally required to keep. You can revoke location permission at any time from the app. You can unlink your WhatsApp number at any time. You can opt out of non-essential notifications.

Children's privacy

GNB Edge is a business tool intended for use by adult employees and contractors of transport organizations. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. We'll revise the effective date above when we do, and material changes will be communicated through the app or by email.
