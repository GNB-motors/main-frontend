What the trip module actually does.

Six jobs on one record, in the order a consignment moves through them.

01
Booking and consignment
Client, route, load and rate captured once, carried through every downstream document.
02
Vehicle and driver assignment
Assign from live availability, with expired documents blocked at source.
03
Trip costing
Freight, fuel, tolls, advances and detention costed against the booked rate.
04
Documents on the trip
LR, e-way bill and invoice generated from the trip, never re-keyed.
05
Market vehicles
Hired vehicles and broker loads costed alongside your own fleet.
06
Route profitability
Margin per trip, route, client and vehicle, refreshed as trips close.
